The Velvet
Heist.

Six guests. One stolen jewel. A very suspicious dinner.

Aster Gallery is hosting an exclusive opening for its historic sapphire, the Midnight Star. At 21:17, the lights go out for ninety seconds. When they return, the jewel in its locked display is an imitation. Nobody has left the building.

How to play the full game
  1. Six guests privately read their character cards (two optional witnesses for 7–8).
  2. Reveal evidence over three rounds, discuss alibis and vote.
  3. Open the sealed solution after everyone makes an accusation.

Sample evidence / 21:08

The Midnight Star is famous for one tiny black fleck. A journalist photographed the display at 21:08, nine minutes before the blackout. In that photograph, the black fleck is already gone.

Question: If the stone was already a fake, why did the security team blame the 21:17 blackout?
